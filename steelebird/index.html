A publisher with a different point of view.Steelebird Books — wordmark study with a restrained, static bird silhouette. No decorative animation.

How the mark works

A flat silhouette sits within the wordmark. There is no simulated flight. The mark should be equally effective on a website, a title page, or a book spine.

Color

Paper, near-black, weathered red. A parent identity intentionally quieter than Re:Read, without feeling corporate.

Shared family

Steelebird can share Re:Read's archival paper language and carefully composed typography while each future imprint keeps its own color and personality.

The strongest test is whether the identity holds together without animation. The small mark should work in one ink color, at thumbnail size, on a title page or the bottom of a book spine.
