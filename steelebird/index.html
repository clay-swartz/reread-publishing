Some stories refuse to stay still.A wordmark first. A real crow silhouette, positioned over the title, briefly lifts away and returns. Its movement uses CSS rather than generated animation.

How the mark works

The bird comes from an actual crow silhouette drawn for NIH BioArt, rather than a fabricated bird. Motion is quiet and intermittent, with a full pause between flights. On print and on book spines, the same mark stays completely still.

Color

Paper, near-black, weathered red. A parent identity intentionally quieter than Re:Read, without feeling corporate.

Shared family

Steelebird can share Re:Read's archival paper language and carefully composed typography while each future imprint keeps its own color and personality.
