Independent publishing

Books worth finding. Books worth keeping.

Steelebird is an independent publishing house for books that deserve more attention—remarkable stories that slipped through the cracks, overlooked work worth revisiting, and original writing with something to say.

We bring an editorial point of view to every edition, from the books we choose to the way they're made and introduced to readers.
