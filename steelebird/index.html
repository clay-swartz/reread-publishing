A bird in the letter.

Three deliberate integrations of your supplied solid bird icons. Rather than placing artwork over the name, each bird becomes the tittle of the “i”; the letter's stem, spacing, and weight are adjusted around it. All three are static, built directly in HTML/SVG.

01 — Soft silhouette / icon 374755

SteelebrdBOOKS

A restrained organic shape replaces the dot, with a narrow custom stem. The most seamless approach if we favor an approachable, quietly eccentric identity.

02 — Geometric / icon 189308

SteelebrdBOOKS

The angular bird is treated as a deliberate typographic terminal, balanced with a slightly wider “i”. More distinctive and abstract.

03 — Flight / icon 967204

SteelebrdBOOKS

A more expressive bird shape, constrained to the scale of a letterform rather than floating separately above the wordmark.
