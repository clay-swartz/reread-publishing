Good books deserve another look.

Steelebird is an independent publishing house guided by curiosity, strong editorial taste, and the conviction that remarkable books are still waiting to be found.

What matters to us

Finding a book is only the beginning.

Some remarkable manuscripts are passed over. Some published books disappear before they reach the readers who would love them.

We want to give those books meaningful attention, from selecting and shaping a title to its design, positioning, and path into readers' hands.

We work with authors, agents, estates, and rights holders to find a sensible way forward for each book. No two projects are exactly alike.

Our approach

A house with a point of view.

Some books stand alone. Others belong together. Steelebird's collections bring distinct voices into conversation through shared themes and considered editions.

American Myths explores stories of America and its contradictions. Other Worlds looks toward literary science fiction and imaginative fiction that challenges what feels familiar.

Authors, agents, rights holders, and publishing partners are welcome.
