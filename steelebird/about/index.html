What We're Building

Steelebird is an independent publisher hellbent on championing timeless books, singular authors, and the rabbit holes they send us down. We're interested in remarkable stories that slipped through the cracks, forgotten favorites worth rediscovering, and new voices with something to say.

But we're building more than a publishing catalog.

We think discovering a great book should be just as much fun as reading one. So we're taking a more editorial approach to publishing, bringing together books, ideas, and people in interesting and sometimes unexpected ways. The goal is to make it easier to follow your curiosity and find something worth reading.

Steelebird is for the readers who have as much fun choosing a book as they do reading one. The ones with TBR lists they'll never finish. And the ones who just want someone to tell them what to read next.

So whether you're looking for something old, something new, or something you didn't know existed, we want to help you find it.
