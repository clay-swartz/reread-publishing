What we're building.

Steelebird is a publishing house with a simple mission: to champion timeless books and singular authors.

We're building a catalog of remarkable books, old and new. We're hunting for out-of-print treasures, long-lost favorites, and extraordinary new work.

Are you an author, agent, or reader with a literary holy grail? A forgotten favorite or something wonderful waiting to be published? We'd love to hear about it. Maybe we can bring it to life.
