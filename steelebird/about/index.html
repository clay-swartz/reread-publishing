Publishing should be an act of discovery.

Good books don't always arrive by the expected route. We believe the right publisher can make all the difference.

Books worth finding.

Steelebird is an independent publishing house for original voices, overlooked stories, and thoughtfully made editions.

What matters to us

Finding a book is only the beginning.

Some remarkable manuscripts are passed over. Some published books disappear before they reach the readers who would love them. Others need a fresh editorial perspective and a reason to be discovered again.

We want to give those books meaningful attention—from selecting and shaping a title to its design, positioning, and path into readers' hands.

We work with authors, agents, estates, and rights holders to find a sensible way forward for each book. No two projects are exactly alike.
