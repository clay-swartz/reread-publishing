About Steelebird

Good books take unexpected routes.

Steelebird is an independent publishing house built to find exceptional stories, shape distinctive editions, and put books in front of the readers who will care about them.

For books
worth finding.

Our point of view

Discovery is only the beginning.

Great books don't always arrive on schedule, fit a familiar category, or get their moment the first time around. Some manuscripts are passed over. Some published books disappear before readers can find them. Others just need someone to look at them differently.

That's where we want to work: with fresh writing that deserves a first chance and overlooked books that have more life in them.

01 / The books

Voice before formula.

We're drawn to distinctive fiction and nonfiction: memorable characters, ambitious ideas, an unexpected angle, or a book that stays with you long after the last page.

02 / The work

Make the edition count.

Editing, design, positioning, and the practical work of reaching readers should be considered together. A book deserves more than a file and a listing.

03 / The people

Good partnerships matter.

We approach authors, agents, estates, and rights holders as partners. Every project is different, and the conversation should start with the book.

Our first imprint

Re:Read is our home for books with something left to say. It champions overlooked work and remarkable original stories, beginning with collections including American Myths and Other Worlds.
