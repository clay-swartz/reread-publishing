Privacy at Steelebird.

What we collect

When you sign up for updates, we collect your email address and the date of your consent. When you contact us, we collect the information in your message. When you submit a book, we collect your contact details, project description, selected collection and any file you attach.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to inquiries, review manuscript proposals and send Steelebird updates to people who have opted in. We do not sell submitted contact information or manuscripts.

Where it's stored

Forms are processed using Supabase. Manuscript attachments are stored in private storage, not on public pages. Service providers may process information as necessary to operate the website.

Your choices

To request access to or deletion of your information, or to withdraw newsletter consent, email clayton.swartz@gmail.com. We retain records only as needed for editorial correspondence and operation of the publishing house, subject to applicable legal requirements.
