Steelebird and the book trade.

A working look at the books we're pursuing, the readers we hope to reach, and how we can make those books practical for independent stores to carry.

The sales conversation

Janine, I appreciated your advice about the real friction of asking a bookstore to open another account, pay another invoice, and cover another shipment. We want to make it easy for booksellers to say yes to a Steelebird title, not create an extra administrative task for them.

We have fulfillment and book-trade ordering infrastructure in place, but we're interested in finding the right commercial and sales relationships for a growing list. Your idea of joining a complementary existing line, where discounts, orders, and freight can be combined, strikes me as especially worth exploring. The right fit would depend on the books and on the economics for everyone involved.

Making the numbers work

Points for discussion, not an announced discount schedule.

With a distribution partner

We would work within that distributor's established trade terms, billing, shipping, and returns arrangements. This is the preferred route if it makes ordering simpler for independent shops.

If Steelebird sells directly

Your suggested 48–50% bookstore discount, no minimum order, and free shipping for independents would be the benchmark to model. We'd need to confirm margins, freight economics, returns, and the practical ordering workflow before offering those terms.

Sales support

Clear seasonal sell sheets, reliable metadata and availability, reader-focused positioning, timely review materials, and direct engagement with specialist communities. A rep should have something specific to say about each book, not just another title to put in front of a buyer.

The working book list

These are projects in discussion or exploration, not signed acquisitions or titles currently available to order. Dates below are original publication years unless noted.

01

Tuyo

Proposed edition
Rachel Neumeier · 2020

The book

A young warrior offered as a sacrifice to an enemy discovers an unexpected alliance. The opening novel in a richly imagined fantasy world, with an established readership.

Our plan

Trade paperback designed for bookstore discovery, with a coordinated series identity and potential special hardcover edition. Reach existing readers while making the series approachable for new ones.

02

Tarashana

Proposed edition
Rachel Neumeier · 2021

The book

Ryo and Aras are drawn into a new threat reaching beyond the familiar borders of their world. A substantial continuation with strong series-reader appeal.

Our plan

Companion trade paperback in the same visual program as Tuyo, supported by series displays, bundled promotions, and a clear reading-order message.

03

Tasmakat

Proposed edition
Rachel Neumeier · 2023

The book

Following Tarashana, Ryo and Aras face a new crisis in the summer country. The third novel in the proposed coordinated set.

Our plan

A third matching trade paperback, allowing booksellers to carry or recommend the three-book sequence together. Collector editions remain an option.

04

Bob Dylan: Things Have Changed

In discussions
Ron Rosenbaum · 2025

The book

A writer and interviewer who spent ten days in conversation with Dylan considers the songs, ideas, and cultural history behind the artist's evolving work.

Our plan

Potential U.S. trade paperback edition, with a new essay under discussion. Position for music, biography, and cultural-criticism shelves, with a clear gift and backlist opportunity.

05

Run from the Hunter

Rights exploration
Charles Beaumont & John Tomerlin · 1957

The book

A tense crime novel about a man fleeing a murder conviction, originally published under the joint pseudonym Keith Grantland.

Our plan

Explore a well-designed, accessible paperback reissue with an emphasis on classic suspense, noir readers, and hand-selling. Rights and edition scope remain under discussion.

06

The Time Mercenaries

Rights exploration
Philip E. High · 1968

The book

In a future where humanity has eliminated its capacity for violence, an ancient submarine crew becomes an unlikely defense against an alien threat.

Our plan

Explore a fresh physical edition for Other Worlds, pairing classic British speculative fiction with thoughtful new packaging. Format and rights would be settled with the representative.

07

London Belle

Manuscript invited
D. F. Jones · Unpublished · written c. 1973–74

The book

A World War II adventure, set in 1940 aboard an aging paddle steamer. Donald Winchester describes a vivid story of rough-hewn and sometimes heroic men, drawing on the author's experience.

Our plan

Read and evaluate the manuscript first. If it fits, develop a standalone physical edition aimed at wartime-history readers and adventure-fiction buyers, with scope and rights still to be discussed.

Also in development: British genre short fiction

We're discussing a sequence of shorter classic speculative-fiction releases with Philip Harbottle, potentially issued as a coordinated series of collectible, recurring editions. Individual stories and rights have not yet been selected, so this is a program concept rather than an orderable title.

Where the collections fit

Steelebird is building curated publishing collections around the interests and passions of readers. The first two are American Myths, exploring and reexamining the American experience, and Other Worlds, devoted to thoughtful, philosophical speculative fiction. We plan additional collections across literary fiction, horror, crime, history, and more, alongside standalone books. Each is an opportunity to approach a specific readership with books, essays, criticism, video, and conversation that go beyond a conventional release campaign.
