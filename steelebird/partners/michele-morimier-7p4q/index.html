Quick answers

What authors and agents usually want to know.

What are you acquiring?

Fiction and nonfiction across genres. We're particularly drawn to singular voices, unexpected premises, unusual histories, and stories with real staying power. Both new work and backlist are welcome.

What rights are you seeking?

Usually North American English-language print and ebook rights, and audio when available. We can discuss narrower arrangements when another publisher already serves certain formats or territories.

What do your deals look like?

Traditional publishing agreements, with modest advances against royalties and clear terms. Each proposal is tailored to the work, existing rights, and publication plan. We prefer straightforward conversations to boilerplate promises.

When would you publish?

We aim to publish within 18 months of signing, with the actual schedule agreed title by title. We want every book to have the time and attention it deserves.

How will the books reach readers?

Through direct sales at steelebird.com and conventional book-trade channels. We're finalizing distribution and fulfillment partners as we assemble the inaugural catalog.

How involved are authors?

We want authors and agents in the conversation on positioning, design, and launch strategy. Steelebird manages the editorial, production, and publishing work, but we don't treat authors like passengers.
