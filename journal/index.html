Before the first book.

A first note on what we're building, what we're looking for, and why we're starting with the search.

We're introducing Re:Read before there's a catalog to browse. That might seem like an odd beginning for a publishing house, but it's a part of the story we want to share.

Some books disappear from view long before they lose their power. Other stories are still waiting to find the right home. We're interested in both: overlooked work that deserves another life and original writing that deserves a first one.

First, find the right stories.

Our first collection is American Myths. We'll look for books and original voices exploring the people, characters, ambitions and legends that shape how America sees itself. We're still researching and acquiring work. We're not announcing titles before they're ready.

Then make them worth keeping.

Finding the right work is only the beginning. Editing and design should respect what makes a book distinctive. That means choices about language, covers, typography, illustration, paper, printing and binding. Not every edition needs the same treatment. We want to make those decisions deliberately, book by book.

And tell you about it.

We plan to use these notes to share the discoveries, decisions and details that don't usually fit on a book jacket. Sometimes that may be a text we've come across. Sometimes it may be a beautiful piece of marbled paper, an argument about a typeface, or the pleasure of a printed page that came out just right.

The first book is still ahead of us. If you'd like to be here when it arrives, we hope you'll follow along.
