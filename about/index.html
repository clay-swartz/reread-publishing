About Re:Read

Great books deserve another chapter.

Re:Read is an emerging independent publishing house dedicated to giving overlooked books new life—and finding original stories worth keeping.

What we publish

Compelling, accessible books with lasting appeal: rediscovered backlist titles, thoughtfully licensed editions, and distinctive new voices.

American Myths

Our first collection explores stories that capture the American imagination. Adventurers, dreamers, outlaws, renegades, and the myths still taking shape.

How we see books

Each edition deserves a reason to exist: fresh editorial perspective, beautiful contemporary design, and a clear invitation for the next generation of readers.

Publishing partnerships

We welcome conversations with authors, agents, estates, and publishers about print reissues, special editions, original work, and selected audiobook opportunities.

Our debut list is in development. Publication dates, distribution arrangements, and future collections have not yet been announced.
