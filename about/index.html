About Re:Read

Great books deserve another chapter.

Re:Read is an independent publishing house hellbent on giving overlooked books a new life. That includes remarkable books that slipped through the cracks and original stories that deserve a first chance.

What we publish

Fiction and nonfiction with a reason to exist on a reader’s shelf. Sometimes that means bringing a neglected book back into print. Sometimes it means backing a new voice or giving a recent book the attention it never received.

American Myths

Our first collection explores stories that capture the American imagination. Adventurers, dreamers, outlaws, renegades, and the myths still taking shape.

How we see books

Making a book available is only the beginning. We care about editing, thoughtful design, clear positioning, and finding readers—especially through bookstores. We believe a publisher should do more than put a file into circulation.

Working with authors

We want publishing relationships built on candid conversations, flexible deal structures, and clear financial terms. Each book is different, and we think its publishing arrangement should reflect that. We welcome conversations with authors, agents, estates, and rights holders.

Have a book we should know about?

We're interested in original manuscripts, overlooked backlist titles, and rights partnerships. Tell us what makes the book worth finding—and who controls the rights, if it has been published before.
