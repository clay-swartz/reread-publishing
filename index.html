What kind of books?

Old favorites that shouldn't have disappeared. New writing that deserves a place on the shelf. Every Re:Read edition starts with a story worth sharing.

Rediscovered.

Overlooked works with something to say today, thoughtfully reintroduced to new readers. Sometimes a great book simply needs another chance.

Original.

Compelling new writing and distinctive voices. We believe the next book worth keeping may be one that hasn't reached readers yet.

From a good story
to a great book.

The words come first. Then the choices that turn those words into an edition someone will want to read, give, and keep.

  1. Acquisition

    We search for overlooked work, discover new voices, and secure the rights needed to publish.

  2. Editorial & design

    We edit with care and shape the cover, type, illustrations, and reading experience around the work.

  3. Printing & binding

    Paper, endpapers, headbands, jackets, and finishes all offer possibilities. We'll choose materials that suit each edition.

  4. Distribution

    We prepare the finished editions to reach the readers and booksellers who will help them find a home.

  5. Into your hands

    The part that matters most: opening the book, reading it, sharing it, and making it yours.

Good books are made together.

Re:Read exists to champion literature and the people who bring it into the world. We want to build relationships with authors, rights holders, designers, and illustrators.

Authors & rights holders

Know an overlooked work that deserves a new edition? Have original writing that might belong with Re:Read? We'd like to know what's out there.

Designers & illustrators

We care about covers, lettering, illustration, print, and the materials of books. We want to meet creative people who share that curiosity.
