AMERICAN MYTHS

America’s literature is full of characters we think we know. We’re looking for the books we don’t.

Not the obvious classics. Not titles you can easily grab anywhere. Re:Read looks for the strange, overlooked, influential and surprising books that make you wonder how you never heard of them.

We organize those discoveries around a theme, research the strongest candidates, and bring the shortlist to our members before the final books are chosen.

American Myths is our first search. The final books are not decided yet — because founding members are part of deciding what deserves to come back.

Help bring the first books back.

Re:Read brings the research, editorial judgment, rights, design and publishing expertise. Members get a real voice in the shortlist and the edition — including the books we choose, covers, paper, binding, endpapers and the themes we explore next.
