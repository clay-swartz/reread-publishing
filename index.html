Think you’ve read everything?

Re:Read finds forgotten American books worth bringing back.

Not the obvious classics. Not another edition of a book already stacked ten-deep at a bookstore. We look for the strange, overlooked, influential and surprising books that make you wonder how you never heard of them.

Every season begins with a larger idea. We research the field, build the shortlist, and bring our members into the process before a book becomes a Re:Read edition.

How a book comes back.

The process is simple on purpose. Re:Read does the digging; members help turn the strongest finds into books worth owning.

01 / Find

We hunt.

Archives, old catalogs, references, dead ends, used-book shelves, forgotten reviews and the trails one book leaves to another.

02 / Curate

We make the case.

Why this book? Why did it disappear? Why is it worth seeing again now? If we can’t answer that, it doesn’t make the shortlist.

03 / Decide

You weigh in.

Members react to the shortlist and help shape the edition — without turning editorial judgment into a popularity contest.

04 / Publish

We bring it back.

A considered new edition with the design, context and physical details the book deserves.
