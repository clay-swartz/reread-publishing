Good to know

Re:Read is part publishing house, part literary club and part collective act of discovery.

What kinds of books are you looking for?

Books that have fallen out of the culture but still have something to say. Some may be public domain. Others may require rights. The common thread is that they are not the obvious classics you can already find everywhere.

What is a drop?

A drop is a themed Re:Read release. We research a larger idea, build a shortlist of discoveries around it, involve members in the final choices, then publish the books that earn their way back.

Is American Myths one book or a collection?

It is the theme for the first Re:Read drop. We are researching multiple books and characters inside that theme now. Founding members will help decide what the final published selection becomes.

How much say do members really get?

A meaningful one. Re:Read keeps editorial responsibility, but members can weigh in on shortlists, themes and physical edition decisions. The goal is collaboration with taste and guardrails — not crowdsourcing every decision.

Are these collector books?

They should be books worth owning. Some releases may eventually have numbered or special editions, but Re:Read is not built only for luxury collectors. The standard edition still has to be thoughtful, physical and compelling.

When does Drop 001 launch?

Coming soon. The research is underway now, and founding members will be invited in before the final selection and edition decisions are locked.
